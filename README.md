**Description**:
Don't you love Halloween? Come over here and get a little nostalgic! I'll even toss in a cool-looking dark-mode skin for the terminal of the text-based, interactive story game. (Sorry, sorry. I know that this game was shipped one day shy of THE day... but Halloween can live on, no? So come on and try it out!) The prompts are straightforward for convenience. They were designed to be self-explanatory. This is a WIP, so we so far only have aesthetic characters for the visual pleasure/elements. Enjoy!!

**The WHY**:
I recently met somebody who desires to produce great video games. He genuinely has talent. Seeing that awesome potential and vibe for his passions inspired me to try it out too. You know that it's good company whenever you can't help but want to join in!
So, here I am. I've never coded anything besides the basic:

... not until TERRA, whose name sounds just like my childhood friend's, popped up in the #announcements channel!! My gosh, within Terra's channels lives the most supportive community. I feel social, for once. There are genuinely people who are trying to help me out, and who are nice to me! I've found my people, and my people are coding. So, so am I. See ya around!
